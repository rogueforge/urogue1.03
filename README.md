UltraRogue is a dungeon game in the line of Rogue, written by Herb
Chong. You choose one of ten character classes, from fighter to ninja,
and go down through a hundred levels of rooms, mazes and throne rooms,
past trading posts and monsters that cast spells and summon others, to
bring back the Amulet of Yendor and the other artifacts.

This is UltraRogue 1.03, dated January 13, 1987.

What is different from 1.02:

- Ten character classes: paladin, ranger, druid, illusionist, assassin
  and ninja join fighter, magician, cleric and thief. Each class loses
  its special abilities in armor or with weapons not its own, and
  spells and prayers come from one pool of points.
- Hundreds of new monsters. Monsters fight each other, wield weapons,
  shoot, cast spells and come in groups with a leader; the undead
  summon lesser undead.
- Familiars, and charmed and friendly monsters.
- A new magic system, with spells listed by cost, and new potions,
  scrolls and wands, among them flying, continuous breathing, shield
  and true sight.
- The game starts in a trading post where the hero buys a kit, and
  trading posts are a menu-driven flea market every 15 levels.
- No limit on experience levels; fighters stun, and thieves, assassins
  and ninjas backstab. Prayer can bring luck, and killing a friendly
  monster costs it.
- Several objects can lie on one square.
- Keypad support, a message of the day and news, and support for
  System V, Xenix and the AT&T 7300.
